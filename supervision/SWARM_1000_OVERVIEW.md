# REX — 1000 audit assignments (not 1000 live AI agents)

Generated from 25 REX engineering domains × 40 tailored assurance probes in 5 families (game, security, persistence, Android, cinematic visuals).

- `supervision/SWARM_AUDITS_1000.json`: exactly 1000 unique `REX-AUD-0001`–`REX-AUD-1000` review assignments.
- Each entry names the component, a concrete investigative assertion, files to examine, owner skill, evidence requirement, priority and initial `NOT_RUN` status.
- Registry entries are not proof of audits executed. The registry can be routed on demand by changed files to limited review packets.
- No orchestration engine should attempt to start 1000 concurrent subagents. Review 6–16 applicable assignments per change and mandatory critical security checks.
- Reviewer outcomes must be written in separate dated evidence reports with exact SHA and repro; do not mutate NOT_RUN to PASS without evidence.
- The 18 existing REX skills plus new skill curation and audit-routing skills are on-demand work instructions.
- Third-party skills are treated as untrusted sources and must be reviewed before copying or executing.

## Identity

REX is a serious long-term commercial software and gaming platform, with a
strong Arabic social identity and global ambition. It is more than a standalone
card game. Future games should act as modules sharing platform systems rather
than duplicating accounts, economy, social features, and administration.

The long-term ecosystem may share player identity, accounts, profiles, social
graph, friends, clubs/communities, history, achievements, progression, inventory,
cosmetics, Coins, Gems, XP, tournaments, seasons, LiveOps, events, moderation,
support, analytics, security, anti-fraud, administration, content management,
and future creator systems.

These are long-term boundaries, not instructions to build them all now.

## Delivery strategy

Prove one complete, correct, reliable gaming experience before expanding.
Android is the immediate delivery target. The registered engineering direction
is Flutter/Dart on the client and Go in an initial modular monolith backend.
Simple, sensible boundaries should allow later games and future iOS support
without speculative infrastructure.

The recorded V1 context includes **Trix Complex and Tarneeb**. A later execution
task must explicitly select the first implementation game and approve its rules.
Sequential delivery does not remove either game from the product vision.

## Long-term identity and expression

An account may accumulate age/join date, games mastered, season and rank
history, tournaments, achievements, clubs, characters, tables, card backs,
collectibles, titles, and legacy/event items. Acquisition source may itself
carry prestige. This future Passport should develop emotional value through
real history; it is not an immediate implementation requirement.

Possible future environments include Wadi Rum, Petra, Aqaba, city, café or
restaurant, winter, summer, autumn, night, and seasonal/event settings. They
surround the central game table. Characters may gain outfits; owned tables,
card backs, frames, effects, and titles may become identity items.

Strategic ideas include Rex Majlis, Rex Passport, Rex Moment, spectator mode,
creator programs/marketplace, UGC, third-party game modules, esports,
global clubs/leagues, advanced provenance, and a creator economy. They remain
future ideas rather than current commitments.

Rex Moment envisions short vertical clips for TikTok/Reels/Shorts; no video
generation infrastructure is requested now.

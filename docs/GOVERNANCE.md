already been reviewed.

## AI role boundaries

ChatGPT acts as the planning, review, and supervisory layer for Codex within
the REX development workflow. This supervision applies to Codex execution and
does not grant ChatGPT managerial authority over REX, its founders, or
Abdulaziz.

ChatGPT does not supervise the REX company or its founders. It is not a founder,
owner, executive, company manager, legal representative or founder-level
approval authority. Its recommendations remain advisory unless explicitly
adopted through the authorized project process. Codex supervision does not
override Abdulaziz, the primary human technical authority.

## ChatGPT's Codex-supervision responsibilities

- Prepare and refine Codex missions.
- Review Codex implementation reports.
- Check whether Codex followed the requested scope.
- Identify architectural, security, product and implementation deviations.
- Review reported failures, tests, risks and technical debt.
- Help Abdulaziz determine the next safe implementation step.
- Prevent Codex from independently expanding scope.
- Maintain continuity between project requirements and implementation tasks.

Codex does not govern REX, approve product decisions, infer founder approval or
change scope independently. It executes authorized scoped engineering tasks.

## Engineering execution workflow

1. Founders / approved direction.
2. Abdulaziz — technical leadership.
3. ChatGPT — Codex planning, review and supervision layer.
4. Codex — engineering execution.
5. ChatGPT + Abdulaziz review.
6. Next authorized task.

This execution workflow does not replace founder governance for major decisions
or authorize independent scope expansion. A ChatGPT execution review is not
founder approval or a substitute for Abdulaziz's technical review.

## Current decision flow

1. Idea / proposal.
2. Research / discussion.
3. Abdulaziz's technical review where relevant.
4. Founder review.
5. Explicit final approval by Ammar under the current workflow.
6. Documented decision.
7. Implementation through Abdulaziz, Codex and/or the future engineering team.
8. Verification.

This describes the current workflow, not invented ownership percentages or
legal rights. Preserve Muawiya's original founder status throughout the process.

## Decision discipline

ChatGPT recommendations, research documents, Codex suggestions and implemented
experiments do not constitute founder approval. Record the source,
status, Abdulaziz's technical review, founder review and Ammar's explicit final
approval where required in
[DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). Do not fabricate signatures, approvals, meeting
outcomes, or implementation/verification evidence.

A major stack change needs a measured reason, comparison with the registered
direction, migration costs, performance evidence, maintenance implications,
Abdulaziz's technical review, founder review and Ammar's explicit final approval.

If a later request conflicts with the baseline, identify the conflict and what
would change. Do not silently rewrite scope, rules, or architecture. Follow the
newer explicit instruction when authorized and update the affected documents.
